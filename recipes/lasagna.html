Here's how to make some lasagna!

Ingredients:

  • Flat lasagna pasta sheets
  • 3 Cups of marinara sauce
  • 2 Cups of Ricotta cheese
  • 3 Cups of shredded Mozarella cheese
  • 1/2 Cup of grated Parmesan cheese

Directions:

  1. Spread a thin layer of red sauce in a 9x13 inch baking dish
  2. Lay down a row of pasta sheets
  3. Spread a layer of ricotta, then mozzarella, and a dusting of Parmesan
  4. Repeat the layers of sauce, pasta, and cheese until the dish is full
  5. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 30 to 40 minutes until golden and bubbly

And voila! Enjoy your Lasagna!
