Here's how to make some spaghetti!

Ingredients:

  • Fresh Spaghetti strands
  • 2 Cups of marinara sauce
  • 1/2 Cup of grated Parmesan cheese

Directions:

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil
  2. Add the spaghetti and cook for 8 to 10 minutes (or 2 minutes if fresh) until al dente
  3. Drain the pasta and save a little starchy cooking water
  4. Toss the hot spaghetti with your warm tomato or meat sauce and top with Parmesan.

And voila! Enjoy your spaghetti!
