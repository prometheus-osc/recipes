Here's how to make some tagliatelle pasta!

Ingredients:

  • Fresh tagliatelle ribbons
  • 1 lb ground beef or ragù sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced) and 1/2 onion (diced)
  • Olive oil

Directions:

  1. Cook the diced onion and minced garlic in olive oil in a pan until soft
  2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned. Add tomato sauce and simmer for 15 minutes
  3. Boil the fresh tagliatelle in a large pot of salted water for 1 to 3 minutes
  4. Drain and mix the tagliatelle directly into the meat ragù sauce

And voila! Enjoy your tagliatelle!
